🌐 Community Harness Scores

A community-driven harness score submission system. Submit your project, get it scanned, and compare your agent-readiness with the community.

1

Vision

vision open-registry community

We envision a public registry where any developer or team can submit their project's Harness Score via GitHub issues. Once submitted, the score is reviewed, verified, and listed so the community can benchmark against each other. This transparent, open-source approach helps raise the bar for agent-readiness across the entire ecosystem. The official leaderboard showcases top-tier curated repos, while the community leaderboard welcomes contributions from anyone who wants to measure and improve their project.

2

How to Submit

guide steps

Follow these simple steps to get your project listed in the community leaderboard:

  1. 1
    Scan your project

    Run harness scan . --json in your project root to generate a complete Harness Score report with subsystem breakdowns.

  2. 2
    Open a GitHub issue

    Go to github.com/iberi22/harness-course/issues and create a new issue using the "Submit Score" template.

  3. 3
    Paste your results

    Include the full JSON output from the scan along with your repository URL. Our maintainers will verify the score and add it to the community list.

  4. 4
    Get listed

    Once approved, your project appears in the community leaderboard below. You can resubmit anytime your score improves!

Benefits of Participating

benefits why

Why should you submit your project to the community registry? Here's what you gain:

🏆 Bragging Rights

Show the world your project's agent-readiness score. A high Harness Score is a badge of quality that sets your project apart.

🔍 Discoverability

Get listed alongside other agent-ready projects. Developers and agents looking for well-structured repos will find yours.

📈 Improve Agent-Readiness

The process of running the scanner and addressing its recommendations directly improves your project's ability to be operated by AI agents.

🌍 Contribute to Standards

Help build open-source agent engineering standards. The more projects in the registry, the better we can define what "agent-ready" means.

Frequently Asked Questions

faq questions

Common questions about the community score registry.

How long does verification take?

Typically 24-48 hours. Our maintainers review your scan output and validate it against your repository before listing.

Can I submit multiple times?

Absolutely! Each time your Harness Score improves, open a new issue with your updated scan. We'll update your listing.

Is my score public?

Yes. All submitted scores are displayed publicly on this page. By submitting, you consent to having your project name, score, and URL listed.

What if my project is private?

We currently only verify public repositories. Private repos cannot be validated by our maintainers.

Can I submit someone else's project?

Only submit projects you own or have explicit permission from the owner. Unauthorized submissions will be removed.
